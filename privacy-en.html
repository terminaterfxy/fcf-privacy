Privacy Policy

Effective: June 1, 2026 · Updated: November 9, 2026

International notice. Terms of Service · 中文版

1. Data controller

Fan Xuyang, Individual Developer · 7@notescience.com · Family Cash Flow (budgeting app with cloud sync and optional family sharing).

2. What we collect

Account data (Apple ID or Google account ID; with Google, the email and name Google provides; with Apple, the name/email you choose to share, which may be a hidden relay address; display ID); financial and household content you enter; device/technical logs for sign-in and sync; suggestions, images and votes you submit in Feedback (see section 6). No ad/analytics SDKs in the current App. Optional App lock stays on your device—we do not receive biometrics.

3. How we use data

To run the App, sync devices, enable family collaboration, secure accounts, fix issues, and comply with law. We do not sell ledger data or use it for third-party ads.

4. Security

  • HTTPS in transit;
  • Encryption at rest on our cloud host;
  • Sign in with Apple or Google + row-level security for account/household isolation;
  • Operator access only when needed for support, security, or legal compliance—not routine browsing;
  • Processors: Apple and Google (sign-in), Supabase (database/sync).

5. Your financial information

We treat ledger data as highly sensitive and protect it with HTTPS, encryption at rest, and strict access controls. Your content is used only to provide the App—not for ads or unrelated purposes—and is not sold to third parties.

Except where required by applicable law or binding lawful demands from competent authorities, we do not review or disclose the specifics of your entries. Family sharing, if enabled, is controlled by whom you invite.

6. Feedback (suggestions and votes)

  • Who can see it: once published, a suggestion's text, images, vote count and status are visible to all signed-in users. Your nickname, display ID and email are not shown. Please do not include ledger figures, contact details or other personal information in a suggestion.
  • Moderation: the developer reads suggestions and marks them “Next update” or “Declined”; declined suggestions are visible only to you.
  • Images: up to 5 per suggestion. They are compressed on your phone and stripped of original metadata such as location (EXIF) before upload, kept in private storage, and viewable only by signed-in users inside the App.
  • Votes: we record which suggestions you voted for, to prevent duplicates and count votes; other users see only the total.
  • Deletion and retention: you can delete a suggestion at any time unless it is in “Next update”. It stops being shown immediately and its images are removed by a scheduled job within about 12 hours, and the text is permanently erased about 7 days after deletion. Deleting your account deletes all your suggestions and votes. Unpublished drafts expire after 30 minutes and their images and text are then removed the same way.

7. International transfers & retention

Data may be stored/processed outside your country. We retain data while you use the App; delete or anonymize after account deletion except where law requires retention.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location: access, rectification, deletion, restriction, portability where applicable, withdraw consent, complain to a supervisor. Contact 7@notescience.com (subject: Family Cash Flow - Privacy).

9. Children

For adults and family use with guardian involvement where appropriate. We cannot verify your age and do not provide age verification. If you are under 14, do not register or use the App alone; use only with a consenting guardian. Contact us to delete a child’s data collected without guardian consent.

10. Governing law

Governed by PRC law; mandatory local consumer rights apply where required. See also Terms of Service.
