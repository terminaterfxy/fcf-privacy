Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page applies to the app Family Cash Flow (家庭现金流) on iPhone and Android. Developer: NoteScience (Fan Xuyang).

Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended, takes effect immediately)

  1. Open Family Cash Flow and go to Manage › Personal Info Management.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  3. If your family still has other members: a member must leave the family first; an admin must remove the other members first, then delete the account.

Option 2: Request by email (if you can't sign in to the app)

From the email address you use to sign in, write to 7@notescience.com with the subject “Family Cash Flow - Delete account”, and include your 8-character display ID if you know it. After confirming the email matches the account, we will delete it within 15 business days and reply to let you know.

Data that is deleted

  • Account and sign-in information: Apple / Google sign-in identifier, email address and name;
  • Nickname, display ID and language preference;
  • Your personal ledger: income, expenses, assets, liabilities, long-term contracts and notes;
  • Feedback you submitted (text and images) and your votes.

Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Data that is kept

  • Entries you recorded in a shared family ledger while in a family belong to the family and remain available to the other members after you leave;
  • Records we are required by law to keep are kept for the legally required period and then deleted.

When it is deleted

  • In-app deletion: your account and personal ledger are deleted immediately;
  • Feedback images are removed within about 12 hours, and feedback text is fully removed within about 7 days;
  • Email requests: completed within 15 business days.

See the Privacy Policy for more details.
